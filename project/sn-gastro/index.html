A Successful Digital Journey for SN Gastro

SN Gastro & Liver Clinic, led by renowned gastroenterologist Dr. Sushil Narang, is a specialized healthcare center in Ahmedabad dedicated to the diagnosis and treatment of gastrointestinal, liver, pancreatic, and digestive disorders. The clinic provides advanced procedures including endoscopy, colonoscopy, liver disease treatment, ERCP, and comprehensive gastroenterology care.

Problem Statement

Even though our client was known as one of the leading organizations within the medical sphere, we needed to identify the problems requiring digital solution:

  • Weak visibility of gastroenterology specialists online
  • Lack of connection between people interested in specialized gastroenterologists and those who offer such services
  • Failure to implement a content strategy aimed at promoting particular procedures conducted in the clinic
  • Need to create a professional site that would make patients feel confident and motivated to contact the company
  • Convenient appointment inquiry process

Our Solution

GRVED formulated a complete digital solution where the major focus was on creating healthcare visibility and providing a patient-friendly experience.

Website Design & User Experience

A sleek and responsive website was developed which was meant to provide a seamless experience across all devices. The web design was done in such a way that ensured the website highlighted services, expertise of doctors, and patient-relevant information.

Keyword-Focused Website Design: A keyword-oriented and service-centered approach was adopted while designing the website. Relevant pages for each service of the clinic were created and include colonoscopy, endoscopy, hepatology treatments, abdominal pain treatments, and digestive disorders.

Patient Friendly Experience: The website was designed in such a way that it included navigation-friendly design, information about different services, contact pages, and booking forms.

Building Trust of Patients: Professional information about doctors, services provided, clinics, and information relevant to patients was included on the website.

Result & Impact

The revolutionary tool has helped SN Gastro in establishing its reputation online:

  • Increase visibility through searches based on gastroenterology
  • Improve experience of users searching for relevant information regarding gastrointestinal health
  • Increase interactions of users with informational content
  • Establish a good reputation in the health care field
  • Increase inquiries and appointments from potential clients

Key Takeaways

  • Web development for modern healthcare
  • SEO-optimized website layout
  • Mobile-friendly site design
  • Content marketing centered around services
  • Lead generation process optimization
  • Improved patient experience

Summary

The SN Gastro case study shows how proper healthcare-focused web development, search engine optimization, and UX design may be used by medical practices in order to grow their online presence. Thanks to a professional online reputation, GRVED helped SN Gastro communicate its knowledge in gastroenterology and hepatology.
